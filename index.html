YOUR FIRST DAY ON THE ROUTE

How to hop

01 / MOVE

One press, one hop.

Use arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen arrows. Move forward, backward, and sideways. Roads have moving cars; grass gives you a safe place to breathe.

02 / DELIVER

Follow the yellow marker.

A house awaits every 15 rows. Land on its marked tile to deliver your parcel and earn 10 coins. Complete that delivery before advancing beyond the checkpoint.

03 / COLLECT

Make every route count.

Each new furthest row earns one distance point. Coins are yours to keep even after a crash. Spend them on hats or a one-hit shield.

04 / BREATHE

No rush. No timer.

Use Pause, P, or Escape to pause and resume. Switching tabs pauses automatically. Traffic gets a little faster after each delivery, up to a safe limit.
